StediTask on this computer

Renaming a column renames its project. Removing a column keeps the project and its tasks. Projects made in StediTask only become columns if you say yes there.

Each project is a Google Tasks list (General is "My Tasks"). Adding, changing, finishing or deleting a task in either place does the same in the other. The time, alarm and CoLab people go in the Google task's notes.

Rules
Snooze on time (optional)

StediMail brings snoozed emails back whenever it's open. To have them come back on time even when it's closed (e.g. on your phone): click Copy helper script, open Google Apps Script, make a new project, paste over everything, save, then choose install in the toolbar and click Run (allow access when Google asks). Once is enough.
